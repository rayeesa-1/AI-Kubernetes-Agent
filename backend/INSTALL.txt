What changed
- Recognized InsForge errors now give specific, fixed public messages.
- Safe logs include upstream HTTP status and a local error category, never
  passwords, email addresses, tokens, or raw upstream response bodies.
- Required email verification is respected even if upstream omits its flag.
- Signup omits redirectTo when email verification is disabled.

Follow the message returned after retrying:
- Account conflict: use Sign in or Verify an existing account.
- Verification redirect: allow http://localhost:3000/ in the InsForge project's
  Authentication redirect settings if local link verification is intended.
- Password policy: follow the requirements shown on your signup form.
- Signup disabled: enable public registration if that is your intended policy.
- Email delivery failed: the account may already be created; fix email delivery
  and use Verify an existing account to resend. Do not recreate the user.
- Generic rejection: share the sanitized backend warning and HTTP status,
  along with the error message. More provider-specific investigation is needed.
